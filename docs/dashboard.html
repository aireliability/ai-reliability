Production AI Maintenance Gate

AI Reliability checks production AI behavior against customer-defined eval specs, validates tool calls and actions, and enforces provider-call budget limits for routed calls.

Scope: Budget enforcement applies to configured provider calls routed through the AI Reliability gate. Direct provider calls that bypass the gate are outside this enforcement scope.
